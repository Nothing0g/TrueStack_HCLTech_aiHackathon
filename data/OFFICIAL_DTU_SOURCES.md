# Official DTU source corpus

Three official DTU source PDFs were supplied for the local project bundle. Page-anchored extracted text sidecars are stored in `data/extracted/` and form part of the starter source corpus. The original large scanned PDFs are included in the local downloadable bundle but excluded from Git history; the public source register preserves the verified provenance URLs.

1. **B.Tech Ordinance / Regulations / Scheme of Teaching & Examinations — B.Tech Program 2025**. Official DTU ordinance page: <https://www.dtu.ac.in/Web/Academics/ordinance.php>. The supplied PDF is a scan; its extracted sidecar uses OCR. Verify any relied-upon clause in the original page.
2. **Eligibility for assessment, examination & evaluation, Odd Semester AY 2026–27**. Examination Branch notice dated 24 August 2026: <https://exam.dtu.ac.in/Notices/Notice_Studnt_O26_240826.pdf>. It concerns exam eligibility in registered/re-registered courses, subject to attendance criteria.
3. **Placement Policy 2025–26**. DTU Department of Training and Placement, dated 3 July 2025, for the 2025–26 passing-out batch: <https://tnp.dtu.ac.in/docs/policy.pdf>. Section 15 says CGPA cutoffs are set by companies; the document does not establish one universal CGPA cutoff for every student or cohort.

## Handling

- The Ordinance and exam notice use OCR-derived text sidecars; the original PDF page is the human verification source.
- Where the Ordinance does not state an effective-from date for the cited clause, the register leaves it blank rather than inventing one.
- The placement policy's passing-out cohort must not be silently generalised to other cohorts.
- The two policy demonstration documents are explicitly synthetic and are never represented as actual DTU issuances.
