NOTICE
(Students)
Subject: Eligibility for assessment, examination & evaluation of courses for Odd Semester, Academic
Session 2026-2027 (as per the registration/re-registration done on the course registration portal)
The students of all semesters are being informed that they:

(a) must download the course registration slip from dashboard on the course registration portal
to be sure of the courses in which they have registered/re-registered.

(b) need to attend classes as per the courses in which they have registered/re-registered.

(c) will be eligible to take examination only in the courses in which they have registered/re-
registered subject to the fulfillment of the attendance criteria.

(d) cannot claim their exam eligibility by just sitting in and attending the classes of the
courses in which they have not registered/re-registered.

(e) require admit cards to appear in Mid Term Examination and End Term Examination(Theory
and Practical). The admit card will be generated strictly as per their course registration/re-
registration status on the Dashboard of course registration portal.

Also note that any last-minute grievance on said subject will not be entertained during Mid Term
Examination and End Term Examination. t
